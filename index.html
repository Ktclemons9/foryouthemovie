Why play? The story in 30 seconds

Katey liked, shared and commented on every post from her favorite creator, because if you show up enough, somebody finally sees you. Then she was chosen: the trip of a lifetime, and a chance to be seen. The fare was a single gold token. She held onto it. Just in case.

Now something is hunting her, her livestream is her only lifeline, and a handful of internet sleuths are racing to reach her. Behind it all is Re, the one who promises followers they'll finally be seen.

What's in it for you? In The Reach, the most devoted get seen. Here, they really do: bring the most friends to the winning flock and you get a speaking line in For You. Every badge you earn unlocks a piece of the story no one else gets to see.
