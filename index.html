3 · SYNOPSIS

Who decides what shows up on your For You page?

Katey did everything right. She liked every post, shared every video, followed every word. And tonight, the reward arrives: a trip of a lifetime, and a promise that everyone she loves will be taken care of.

She goes live to say goodbye. She wakes up in a kill room. The camera is still rolling. One stranger is watching. In a panic, they send her stream to a small-town hero and his group of internet sleuths, and the race to find her begins. But Katey isn't trapped by a killer. She's trapped by Re, the thing behind every cult leader in history, and now behind the influencers on your screen. Somewhere in the house, a door is waiting. Every crossing has a fare. No one who pays it has ever come back.

And the longer the sleuths watch, the more they understand. Re isn't only coming for Katey.

It's coming for whoever's watching.
