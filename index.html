HOW TO CLIMB
  1. Share your link below. It's yours alone.
  2. Post it anywhere: Facebook, an Instagram story, your TikTok bio, or a text to a friend.
  3. When a friend follows through your link, or you check in each day, you rise closer to Re. Clicks don't count. Only people who follow.
