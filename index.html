THIS MONTH'S REWARD
Counting this month's followers...
#1 recruiter: a real gold token in the mail. Top 10: names in the end credits.
Reward rules
  • Each calendar month is its own contest. It ends at 11:59 PM Eastern on the last day of the month.
  • Rewards are given only if at least 100 new people follow during that month. If the goal isn't met, the rewards roll over to the next month.
  • Rankings count people who follow through your personal invite link during that month.
  • To be eligible, you must add your email when you choose your name. Your email is private and is used only to contact winners.
  • The #1 recruiter receives a gold token by mail. The top 10 are named in the end credits of For You, under the name they choose.
  • Ties go to whoever reached that number first.
  • Open to US residents 18 and older. No purchase necessary. Following is free.
  • Winners are contacted by email within 7 days of the month's end and have 14 days to reply, or the reward passes to the next person.
  • Fake or duplicate accounts are disqualified.
