THE STORY

All she had to do was pay the fare.

From the mind of writer-director Keshia Clemons.

Katey liked, shared and commented on every post from her favorite creator, because if you show up enough, somebody finally sees you. Then the invitation came: the trip of a lifetime, and a chance to be seen. The fare was a single gold token.

She held onto it. Just in case.

She wakes up somewhere she doesn't recognize. The doors don't lead where they should, something is hunting her, and the only thing still working is her livestream.

A handful of internet sleuths find her feed. Larry, the hero of the Denver Miracle, and his team can see everything, but nothing they send gets through. She keeps asking if anyone is there. Someone named Re keeps answering.

Every clue leads to a group that's been scrubbed from the internet: The Reach. And the one man who tried to warn the world says it was never about the leaders. Something has always stood behind them, and tonight it wants what Katey kept.

They can see her. Now they have to reach her before it does.
